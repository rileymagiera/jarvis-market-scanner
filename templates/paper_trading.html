How to read this page

A first-time visitor should be able to understand the purpose and limits of this information without knowing trading jargon.

What you are seeingJarvis organizes saved market observations into research clues, candidate rankings and measured outcomes.
How to interpret itLook for several pieces of evidence agreeing—price trend, trading activity, relative strength and data quality. A score is a ranking aid, not a probability of profit.
What the status meansVerified means the data passed defined checks. Provisional, delayed, stale or unverified means confidence is limited and should be treated cautiously.
What it does not meanResearch rankings and historical results are not guarantees, forecasts or automatic trade instructions. Jarvis does not place live orders.

Risk and execution rules

Entry eligibilityOfficial BUYs, or an isolated Risk-Off experiment when the market-regime gate is the only failed readiness condition. Both require all other gates, verified live pricing, and the saved entry range.
Position sizingThe official score scales modeled risk up to 1% of account equity per trade. Each position is capped at 25% of equity, with four open positions maximum.
Exit handlingSell 50% when target 1 is first observed, the rest at target 2, or the full remaining position at the stop.
CostsCoinbase One Basic regular-app trades use $0 eligible trading commission within $500 monthly volume; the simulator estimates a 0.50% spread per side and tracks monthly volume. Standard fees after the allowance are not yet configured, so returns beyond the cap may be overstated. Webull U.S. stocks use $0 commission plus modeled CAT/SEC pass-through fees.
This is a scan-based paper simulation, not a tick-by-tick backtest. If a scan misses a price crossing between scans, the ledger records only what the next verified observation shows. Open-position P/L deducts entry fees and estimated exit fees; actual spreads and fees can differ, and Coinbase standard fees after the monthly allowance are not configured. Starting capital is fixed at $250.00.
