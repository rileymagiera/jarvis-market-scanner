Capital allocation queue

Official score is a signal-strength rating, not a win probability. Paper confluence labels observed setup evidence, not a promised win rate. Target-2 R/R compares gross modeled reward at target 2 with modeled loss at the stop. Net target ladder R/R deducts current modeled fees and spread from the planned 50%-at-target-1 and 50%-at-target-2 outcome versus a full stop. This is a hypothetical payoff comparison, not a forecast win rate. Paper allocation is virtual money assigned by the simulation—not a broker investment.

Official BUYs and qualifying Risk-Off experimental entries are ranked first; enabled early-entry experimental signals are considered after them. All share the same virtual cash and existing sizing limits. The official score scales modeled risk up to the existing 1% maximum. Among eligible early-entry experiments, confirmed trend-and-volatility confluence is considered before score; no official BUY priority changes. When experimental trades have identical score and saved target-2 R/R, fee-adjusted target-ladder R/R breaks their tie. Deferred signals are reconsidered on later scans. Risk and execution rules

Entry eligibilityOfficial BUYs require every BUY gate. A separate Risk-Off experiment may qualify only when the market-regime gate is the sole failed BUY check. When enabled, early-entry experiments have their own verified-price, regime, and saved entry requirements; they are not official BUYs. Every entry must meet the existing account and sizing limits.
Position sizingThe official score scales modeled risk up to 1% of account equity per trade. Each position is capped at 25% of equity, with four open positions maximum.
Exit handlingSell 50% when target 1 is first observed, the rest at target 2, or the full remaining position at the stop.
CostsCoinbase One Basic regular-app trades use $0 eligible trading commission within $500 monthly volume; the simulator estimates a 0.50% spread per side and tracks monthly volume. Standard fees after the allowance are not yet configured, so returns beyond the cap may be overstated. Webull U.S. stocks use $0 commission plus modeled CAT/SEC pass-through fees.
Net target ladder R/R uses a $100 reference notional for fee modeling; it does not allocate $100, guarantee both targets, or estimate win probability. It applies the existing Coinbase or Webull fee assumptions. This is a scan-based paper simulation, not a tick-by-tick backtest. If a scan misses a price crossing between scans, the ledger records only what the next verified observation shows. Open-position P/L deducts entry fees and estimated exit fees; actual spreads and fees can differ, and Coinbase standard fees after the monthly allowance are not configured. Starting capital is fixed at $250.00.
