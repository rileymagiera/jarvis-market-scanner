How to use Jarvis Home

Home provides a quick summary and shortcuts. Market Overview is the full scanner. Signal Lab explains the research. Paper Trading is virtual only. Runner Impact uses saved verified observations to compare each research candidate's best recorded gain with its latest return, both measured from the same tracking baseline. Those are not simulated or executed trades. Signal research is not a trade authorization.

Jarvis is a research system. Snapshot metrics can be delayed. No live brokerage orders are placed here.
