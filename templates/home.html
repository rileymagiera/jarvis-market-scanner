How to use Jarvis Home

Home provides a quick summary and shortcuts. Market Overview is the full scanner. Signal Lab explains the research. Paper Trading is virtual only. The illustrated chart above is decorative, not market data. Signal research is not a trade authorization.

Jarvis is a research system. Snapshot metrics can be delayed. No live brokerage orders are placed here.
