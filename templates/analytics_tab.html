How to read this page

A quick guide for anyone opening Jarvis for the first time. The numbers below are research clues—not instructions to buy or sell.

1 · Start with the summaryThe cards at the top count the records and measurements available for this page. They describe the saved scan, not the entire market.
2 · Read the evidence togetherCompare price movement, trading activity, trend and data quality. One strong number by itself does not confirm a good trade.
3 · Check the price statusVerified means the price passed Jarvis’s checks. Last known, delayed or unverified means confidence is lower; do not treat it as a confirmed live quote.
4 · Understand the limitsHistorical returns and research scores describe observed data. They do not predict future performance or automatically create a BUY signal.
